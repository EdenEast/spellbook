# Lenses

Read when exploring directions. Pick the one or two lenses that fit the idea.
Running every lens produces many shallow ideas; a few considered directions are
worth more.

## Generate directions

- **Reframe the problem.** Write "How might we <outcome> for <person> without
  <constraint>?" Try two or three framings. A framing with a solution inside it
  ("How might we build a chatbot") is still anchored; pull back to the problem.
- **The job behind the ask.** "When I <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I
  can <outcome>." The real competitor is whatever they do today instead.
- **Ten times simpler.** What is the version with one feature, zero
  configuration, or half the steps? Often it is a list, a script, or a
  checklist rather than an app.
- **Invert it.** What if the user did the work instead of the system, or the
  other way round? What if the steps ran in the opposite order?
- **Remove a constraint.** What would they build with no time or budget limit?
  Then ask which part of that answer survives the real limits.
- **Borrow a structure.** Which other domain already solved a structurally
  similar problem? Match the structure, such as "trust between strangers",
  not the surface, such as "Uber for X".
- **Change the scale.** What if this had ten users? A million? Just the user?

## Stress-test a direction

- **Painkiller or vitamin.** Does it solve a frequent, felt problem, or make
  something slightly nicer? Signs of a painkiller: the user describes the
  problem with feeling, or has already built a workaround.
- **Hardest part.** Name the hardest piece to build. Is it a known-hard problem
  or a new one? Does it depend on something they do not control?
- **Pre-mortem.** It shipped and nobody used it. Why? Each plausible reason is
  either an assumption to check or a sign the direction should change.
- **Sort the assumptions.** Must be true, or the idea fails. Should be true, or
  the approach changes. Might be true, which can wait. Check the "must" pile
  first, ideally with a spike.
- **What it is not.** A direction without a "not doing" list has not been
  chosen yet.
