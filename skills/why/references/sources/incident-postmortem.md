# Incident & Postmortem Context

Apply these signals only within your assigned source. Treat names below as
examples; discover the actual labels, channels, fields, and permissions first.
Record links into other sources under Additional Leads for the coordinator.

- **Source control:** incident-linked fixes, defensive checks, reverts, and reapplications in commits and PR discussions.
- **Issue trackers:** incident, severity, postmortem, and reliability labels or linked action items.
- **Documents:** incident timelines, postmortems, and reliability design notes.
- **Team chat:** incident channels and discussions near the target's introduction date.
- **Infrastructure observability:** incident or monitor timelines, alerts, and correlated logs around that date.
- **Error tracking:** linked incidents, exception spikes, and first-seen or resolved releases.
- **Analytics warehouses:** verified incident datasets, query failures, and usage changes around the incident window. Discover tables and columns before querying.
